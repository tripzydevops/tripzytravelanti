### Pillar 1: Direct Deal & Booking Transaction Commissions (8% – 15%)
* **Mechanism:** When users discover and redeem hotel stays, restaurant dining vouchers, beach club passes, or yacht charters on Tripzy, Tripzy retains an **8% to 15% marketplace commission** on gross booking volume (GMV).
* **Payment Flow:** Seamlessly processed via Turkish payment gateways (Iyzico / PayTR) with automated split payouts to merchants.

---

### Pillar 2: The Viral Flash Lottery & "Consolation Voucher" Engine
* **The Traditional Giveaway Flaw:** In typical lotteries, 99.9% of entrants lose, receive nothing, and churn.
* **The Tripzy Conversion Funnel:** 
  1. 10,000 users share the lottery to Instagram Stories to win 1 free night at a boutique hotel.
  2. The cryptographic draw selects 1 verified winner via SHA-256.
  3. **The 9,999 non-winners automatically receive a 48-Hour Consolation VIP Voucher** (e.g., *“You didn't win the free stay, but here is an exclusive 25% discount valid for 48 hours”*).
  4. At a conservative **3% conversion rate**, that single free lottery generates **300 immediate paid bookings**, delivering **₺150,000+ in merchant revenue** and **₺22,500 in net commission to Tripzy**.

```
  10,000 Entrants ──► 1 Grand Prize Winner
         │
         └──► 9,999 Non-Winners ──► Consolation Voucher (25% off / 48h)
                                         │ (3% Conversion Rate)
                                         ▼
                               300 Immediate Paid Bookings
                               (₺22,500 Net Commission to Tripzy)
```

### Pillar 3: Google AdMob & Rewarded Video Ads Engine (Ödüllü Video Reklamlar)
* **The Model:** Users choose to watch a 15–30 second sponsor video to earn **+1 Instant Flash Lottery Ticket** or **unlock a secret 5% extra discount voucher**.
* **Why Google Rewarded Ads Excel:**
  * **100% Policy Compliant:** Google explicitly supports opt-in rewarded video formats for digital tokens/tickets.
  * **Daily Cap (3 videos/day):** Protects user experience and maintains peak advertiser eCPMs.
  * **VIP Member Exemption:** Tripzy VIP subscribers get all bonus tickets automatically **without ads**, heavily boosting VIP subscription conversions.
* **The Math & eCPM Breakdown:**
  * **Turkish Rewarded Video eCPM:** **$2.50 – $6.00** per 1,000 views.
  * **Foreign Tourist / Expat eCPM in Turkey:** **$15.00 – $35.00** per 1,000 views.
  * **Projected Revenue:** 20,000 daily ad impressions @ $4.50 blended eCPM = **$2,700/mo (~₺92,000/mo)**. At 100k MAU, this scales to **$13,500/mo (~₺460,000/mo)** in pure passive profit.

### Pillar 5: Merchant Monetization & The 3-Stage Evolution

To balance rapid onboarding during launch with aggressive monetization at scale, merchant monetization evolves through three defined phases:

```
  PHASE 1: LAUNCH (Months 1–3)      PHASE 2: TRACTION (Months 4–6)      PHASE 3: SCALE (Month 6+)
┌───────────────────────────────┐ ┌───────────────────────────────┐ ┌───────────────────────────────┐
│     100% PURE BARTER          │ │    PERFORMANCE HYBRID         │ │    FEATURED AD ENGINE         │
│ • ₺0 upfront fee to hotel     │ │ • ₺0 upfront fee              │ │ • Upfront "Flash Drop" fee    │
│ • Hotel gives 1 free room/meal│ │ • Hotel gives free inventory  │ │   (₺2,500 – ₺5,000)           │
│ • Tripzy monetizes non-winner │ │ • Tripzy takes 15% commission │ │ • Guaranteed 5k+ story shares │
│   Consolation Vouchers & ads  │ │   on Consolation Bookings     │ │ • Bidding for weekend slots   │
└───────────────────────────────┘ └───────────────────────────────┘ └───────────────────────────────┘
```

#### Why Phase 1 is Free Barter:
Hotels in Turkey have empty rooms mid-week with near-zero marginal cost. Asking for free inventory is painless. Tripzy uses this free inventory to acquire tens of thousands of users and followers at zero cash cost.

#### Why Phase 3 Converts to Paid Featured Drops:
A boutique hotel in Bodrum or Antalya spends ₺25,000–₺50,000/month on Meta ads with low conversion. When Tripzy delivers **5,000 real people posting about the hotel on Instagram Stories**, paying a **₺2,500 listing fee** is an obvious $10\times$ ROI upgrade over Meta ads.

## 4. Cost Structure & High-Efficiency Infrastructure

To fulfill the core architectural requirement of serving 100k+ MAU with minimal operational overhead:

1. **Database & Infrastructure (Supabase & Cloudflare):** Connection pooling, edge caching, and Supabase PostgreSQL vector search maintain database costs below **$100 – $250 / month**.
2. **AI Reasoning (Gemini & FastAPI):** Batch embeddings and local rule fallbacks keep LLM inference costs under **$75 / month**.
3. **Vision OCR Verification:** Lightweight, high-throughput OCR microservice processes 50,000 story screenshots for under **$50 / month**.
4. **Total Fixed Infrastructure Cost:** **$250 – $400 / month (~₺8,500 – ₺13,500)**, enabling gross margins above **90%**.

## 5. Regulatory & Legal Architecture (Turkey MPİ & KVKK)

* **Milli Piyango İdaresi (MPİ) Exemption:** Flash lottery campaigns are structured as **Commercial Loyalty & Promotional Programs (Promosyon / Ticari Sadakat Kampanyası)**. Because every user has a 100% free entry method (social share / rewarded ad / referral code) and vouchers represent commercial service discounts rather than cash gambling, campaigns do not trigger gambling licensing taxes.
* **KVKK (Personal Data Protection) Compliance:** Explicit consent checkboxes for phone/WhatsApp deal notifications are logged with cryptographic timestamps in `public.user_consents`.
